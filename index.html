About this project

Experimental structured identity

This website is an experimental prototype exploring how identity information and credentials can be represented in structured, machine-readable form. It demonstrates concepts including provenance, verification, permissions, and trusted data for AI systems.

The underlying concept is related to an experimental SBT / structured identity framework, but the emphasis here is machine-readable identity rather than cryptocurrency or NFTs.

This is not a government-issued or institution-issued digital identity.

Privacy & permissions

Machine-readable does not mean fully public

A mature system could support field-level permissions, selective disclosure, public and private claims, and different visibility levels for different audiences.

This public demo intentionally includes only public-safe information.
